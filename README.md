# My Favorite Series: The Summer I Turned Pretty

## Introduction
My favorite series is **The Summer I Turned Pretty**, a romantic drama series based on the novel trilogy written by Jenny Han.

## About the Series
The story follows Isabel "Belly" Conklin, a teenage girl who spends her summers at Cousins Beach with her family and the Fisher brothers, Conrad and Jeremiah. As she grows older, her relationships become more complicated as she experiences love, heartbreak, friendship, and self-discovery.

## Why I Love This Series
- The romantic and emotional storyline.
- The beautiful summer setting at Cousins Beach.
- The chemistry between the characters.
- The realistic experiences of growing up and falling in love.
- The amazing soundtrack that matches every scene.

## Main Characters
1. **Isabel "Belly" Conklin** - The main character who experiences love and personal growth.
2. **Conrad Fisher** - The quiet and mysterious older Fisher brother.
3. **Jeremiah Fisher** - The cheerful and outgoing younger Fisher brother.
4. **Steven Conklin** - Belly's older brother.
5. **Taylor Jewel** - Belly's best friend.

## Favorite Things About the Series
> "Everything good, everything magical happens between the months of June and August."

I love how the series captures the feeling of summer, first love, unforgettable memories, and the challenges of growing up.

## My Rating
**10/10** - A beautiful and emotional series that makes me laugh, cry, and feel nostalgic.

## Conclusion
The Summer I Turned Pretty is my favorite series because it reminds me that growing up comes with unforgettable memories, unexpected changes, and complicated feelings. It is a perfect series for anyone who enjoys romance, friendship, and coming-of-age stories.
